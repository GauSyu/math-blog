I am [Gau-Syu](https://gausyu.top/) (高煦), an obscure redshirt quietly tinkering with mathematics. 
For me, mathematics is a human activity, and hence its value lies in what it helps people know and understand. In the age of AI, I believe that mathematical papers whose sole contribution is a proof of a conjecture or the solution of an open problem no longer have much significance. For this reason, unless a full paper is required, I would rather post my mathematical ideas and proof sketches here than write one. I am thus restarting Ecole π, my personal math blog, with this aim. I will try to explain my ideas clearly enough, such that any expert or an average AI model is able to expand them easily into completed papers, while making the ideas useful to human readers.

The [old blog](https://gausyu.top/MathBlog/) is still available.
